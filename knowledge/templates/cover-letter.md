# Cover Letter 模板

期刊投稿必备（顶会一般不需要）。审稿人不一定细看，但 AE / EIC 通常会扫一眼。
长度：≤ 1 页。

## 标准结构

```
[Your Address]
[Date]

Dear Dr. [Editor Last Name],

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "[Full Title]," for consideration as
a [Research Article / Letter / Brief Communication] in [Journal Name].

[Paragraph 1: Problem & Significance — 3-4 sentences]
[Paragraph 2: Our Contribution — 3-4 sentences]
[Paragraph 3: Why this journal — 1-2 sentences]
[Paragraph 4: Statements — originality, conflicts, suggested reviewers]

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere,
and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved
the submission and declare no conflicts of interest.

[If asked: list of suggested reviewers / non-preferred reviewers]

Thank you for considering our work. We look forward to your response.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding Author Name, on behalf of all authors]
[Affiliation, Email]
```

## 段落写作指引

### Paragraph 1: Problem & Significance

回答："这个问题为什么重要？"

> Recent advances in $\{field\}$ have enabled $\{capability\}$, with applications in
> $\{domain 1\}$ and $\{domain 2\}$. However, $\{specific open problem\}$ remains a major
> obstacle to $\{real-world impact\}$. Addressing this requires $\{what is needed\}$,
> which has been challenging due to $\{technical reason\}$.

### Paragraph 2: Our Contribution

回答："你做了什么？取得什么结果？"

> In this work, we propose $\{Method Name\}$, $\{one-sentence description\}$.
> Our key insight is $\{insight\}$, which enables $\{capability\}$.
> Through extensive experiments on $\{N\}$ benchmarks ($\{D_1, D_2, ...\}$), we demonstrate
> that $\{Method\}$ achieves $\{X\%\}$ improvement over $\{strongest baseline\}$, while
> reducing $\{compute / data\}$ by $\{Y\%\}$. We further show $\{additional finding\}$.

### Paragraph 3: Why This Journal

回答："为什么是这个期刊？"

> The interdisciplinary nature of this work — combining $\{A\}$ and $\{B\}$ — aligns
> well with the scope of $\{Journal\}$, and we believe it will be of interest to your
> readership of $\{audience\}$.

### Paragraph 4: Disclosures

> - This manuscript is original and not under review elsewhere.
> - All authors approved the submission and contributed substantially (see CRediT statement).
> - We declare no competing interests / We declare the following conflicts: $\{...\}$.
> - Code and data will be / are released at $\{URL\}$.
> - This work was funded by $\{grant\}$.

## 建议 reviewer / 排除 reviewer（如期刊允许）

```
**Suggested reviewers:**
1. Prof. A — expert in $\{topic\}$ at $\{affiliation\}$ (a@x.edu)
2. Dr. B — author of [REF] cited in our work (b@y.org)
3. Prof. C — independent perspective from $\{adjacent field\}$ (c@z.edu)

**Non-preferred reviewers (with brief reason):**
- Prof. D — direct collaborator within past 4 years
```

不要：建议自己导师 / 同实验室 / 近期合作者 / 弟子。期刊会查。

## 不同期刊偏好

| 期刊 | Cover Letter 偏好 |
|------|-------------------|
| **Nature 系**（NatureMI / NeurIPS-track Nature） | 强调 broad impact、interdisciplinary、为什么 Nature 而不是专业期刊 |
| **Science 系** | 同上，更强调"为什么现在 / 突破性" |
| **TPAMI / JMLR** | 技术贡献 + 与现有工作差异，弱化 hype |
| **IEEE Transactions** | 简洁、严谨，列出所有相关 IEEE 论文 |
| **Cell / Nature Medicine** | 临床转化潜力、与现有 SOTA 直接对比 |
| **专业小期刊** | 直接、不过度推销 |

## 红线

- ❌ 用 ChatGPT 生成且不修改 — AE 一眼能认出（特定句式 / "delve into" 等）
- ❌ 同一篇文章给多个期刊投相同 cover letter
- ❌ 夸大 claim（"first ever"、"revolutionary"），与 abstract 不符
- ❌ 字数超过 1 页
- ❌ 拼错主编名字或期刊名（**最致命**）
- ❌ 提到拒稿历史（"Previously rejected by X, we have revised..."）

## 检查清单

- [ ] 主编姓名、期刊名 100% 准确
- [ ] 单页内
- [ ] 包含 originality / conflicts 声明
- [ ] 与摘要核心 message 一致
- [ ] 若有 cover letter 题目要求（如 "highlights" / "significance"）已满足
- [ ] PDF 格式、统一字体
- [ ] 投稿系统其他字段（impact statement、cover image）单独准备
